Moving Clouds Over Mountains With 10-Stop ND

Keep the mountain or foreground sharp while clouds stretch and blur across the sky.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 4 sec | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Look for a mountain scene with obvious cloud movement.
  • Include a strong still subject such as a ridge, trees, lake edge, or foreground rock.
  • Compose and focus before putting on the 10-stop ND filter.
  • Put the AF point on a sharp mountain edge, tree line, or rock.
  • Attach the filter and take a few versions with different shutter speeds.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a sharp mountain edge, tree line, or rock - not clouds.

Look for: The solid subject should look sharp; only clouds should blur.

If this happens

Clouds are not blurredTry a longer shutter speed, such as 8 seconds.
Mountain is blurry tooSecure tripod and use 2-second self-timer.
Sky is too brightTry -1/3 exposure compensation.
Picture looks flatReframe with stronger foreground or mountain shape.

Tips

  • Cloud blur works best when clouds move across the frame, not toward camera.
  • Take one at 2 seconds, one at 4 seconds, and one at 8 seconds.
