Small Trip Details

Small details become useful memories when you photograph one thing clearly: a ticket, boot, map, coffee, sign, or souvenir.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Choose one detail that says something about the day.
  • Move close and remove extra clutter from the edges.
  • Put the AF point on the most important word, texture, or object edge.
  • Half-press and confirm that detail is sharp.
  • Take one tight frame and one with a little context around it.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the most important word, texture, or object edge.

Look for: The chosen detail should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Detail is softRefocus on the nearest important edge and hold still.
Frame is clutteredMove or remove one distracting object from the edge.
Text is unreadableMake one straight-on frame with the label parallel to the camera.
Photo feels randomInclude a small bit of context such as a hand, table, or map.

Tips

  • These small pictures often become the best memory prompts later.
  • Photograph labels and signs straight on when you want to read them later.
