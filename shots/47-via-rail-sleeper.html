Shot 47 | Day 10 | September 26 | VIA Rail The Canadian

VIA Rail Sleeper - Before You Settle In

Photograph the sleeper space before bags, coats, and bedding hide what the train room looked like.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Take the picture soon after boarding, before unpacking.
  • Stand as far back as the available space safely allows.
  • Include the berth, window, and enough surrounding interior.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp berth, wall, or window-frame edge.
  • Take one wide overview and one detail such as the berth number or window.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a clear edge in the middle of the sleeper area.

Look for: The sleeper details should look crisp, rather than luggage close to the lens.

If this happens

Room looks distortedBack up safely instead of holding the camera inches from an object.
Dim train is blurryBrace the camera and take another frame.
Window is white and room darkEmphasize the room rather than perfectly exposing inside and outside.
Luggage blocks the viewTake the overview before unpacking or move one easy item.

Tips

  • Take the overview immediately after boarding.
  • Also photograph a small detail such as the berth number.
