Calm Lake Surface With 10-Stop ND

Keep the shoreline or main subject sharp while the water becomes smoother and calmer.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 2 sec | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Look for a lake with small ripples or light wave texture.
  • Compose using shoreline, mountain, dock, or rocks as the stable subject.
  • Focus before putting on the 10-stop ND filter.
  • Put the AF point on a sharp still object near the water.
  • Attach the filter and take versions at different shutter speeds.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a sharp shoreline, dock, rock, or mountain edge - not water.

Look for: The solid object should look sharp before taking the picture; water may blur.

If this happens

Water stays choppyTry a longer shutter speed, such as 4 seconds.
Whole photo is blurryUse tripod and 2-second self-timer.
Camera will not focusRemove filter, focus first, then put it back on.
Picture is boringReframe with a stronger subject such as rocks, dock, or reflection.

Tips

  • Compose and focus first, then put the filter on.
  • Take one at 1 second, one at 2 seconds, and one at 4 seconds.
