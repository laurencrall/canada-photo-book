Shot 11 | Day 8 | September 24 | Moraine Lake

Moraine Lake - Classic Rockpile View

Make sure the lake and the Ten Peaks both fit cleanly in the frame before the light changes.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • From the Rockpile viewpoint, check all four edges before shooting so the lake and peaks fit cleanly.
  • Keep the lake low enough to show its shape, but do not crop the peaks.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp peak edge, not on smooth water.
  • Half-press and confirm the mountain edge looks sharp before pressing fully.
  • Take several frames as the late-afternoon light changes, but protect the last-shuttle timing.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp peak edge, not on smooth water.

Look for: The peak under the focus box should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Lake is cut offStep back or widen the view until the recognizable lake shape fits cleanly.
Peaks are not sharpMove the AF point to a crisp peak edge and refocus.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation, then compare with 0.
People fill the viewKeep the camera position and take several frames; choose the cleanest one later.

Tips

  • Moraine is the priority stop on the shuttle day, so protect enough time for it.
  • Do not risk missing the last shuttle for a few more frames.
