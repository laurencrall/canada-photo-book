Shot 18 | Day 9 | September 25 | Jasper

Pyramid Lake - Moonlit Night Landscape

At night, keep the scene simple and steady: Pyramid Mountain, the lake, and moonlit sky are enough.

Start here

A+ | Mode A+ | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Go only if you are rested, the sky is clear, and you have a safe place to stand.
  • Use a simple lake-and-mountain composition with as little extra clutter as possible.
  • Put the AF point on a bright mountain edge or distant light, not on the dark sky.
  • Half-press and confirm focus; if it will not confirm, use a brighter edge at the same distance.
  • Brace the camera on a rail, wall, or other solid support and take several frames.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a bright mountain edge or distant light, not on the dark sky.

Look for: The focus box should confirm on the bright edge before you press fully.

If this happens

Camera will not focusAim at a brighter mountain edge or distant light at the same distance.
Picture is blurryBrace against something solid and take several frames.
Scene is too darkUse A+ for a quick safe frame; keep the scene simple.
Too tired or cloudySkip the outing; the daytime Jasper stops are the priority.

Tips

  • This is a moonlit landscape, not a deep-dark Milky Way night.
  • Do not walk near water or uneven ground in the dark for a picture.
