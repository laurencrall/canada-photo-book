Shot 09 | Day 7 | September 23 | Lake Louise

Lake Louise - Canoe Detail

Make the canoe the clear subject and let the open water stay simple around it.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/500 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Wait for one canoe or a small group to separate from other boats.
  • Place the canoe off-center and leave open water in front of it.
  • Put the AF point on the canoe edge, paddle, or a person in the boat, not on the water.
  • Half-press and confirm the canoe is sharp before pressing fully.
  • Take several frames as the canoe moves into a cleaner patch of water.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the canoe edge, paddle, or a person in the boat, not on the water.

Look for: The canoe under the focus box should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Canoe is blurryMake sure Tv shows 1/500 or faster and take another frame.
Focus lands on waterMove the AF point to a canoe edge or paddle and refocus.
Canoe is tinyWait for it to come closer or zoom in; you do not need the whole lake.
Canoe is too darkTry +1/3 exposure compensation and compare with 0.

Tips

  • You do not need to include the whole lake; the canoe is the picture.
  • Take a few frames so you can choose the best paddle position later.
