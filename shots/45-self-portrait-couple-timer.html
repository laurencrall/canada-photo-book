Self-Portrait / Couple Photo With the Timer

Set up and focus the picture before starting the timer, then let the camera take several frames automatically.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Put the camera securely on the tripod and leave enough room for both people.
  • Have one person stand in position while the other focuses on that face.
  • Set the 10-second continuous self-timer for about five shots.
  • Press the shutter fully, then walk into position during the countdown.
  • Stay in place while the camera takes the series, then check the results.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the face or nearest eye of the person already standing in position.

Look for: The face should look sharp and focus should confirm before starting the timer.

If this happens

Scenery is sharp but people are blurryPut one person in position, focus on their face, and refocus.
Not enough time to get into placeMake sure the 10-second timer is selected, not 2 seconds.
Odd expressionsUse continuous self-timer so the camera takes several pictures.
Faces are darkTry +1/3 exposure compensation and take another set.

Tips

  • Leave extra room around both people so heads or feet are not cut off.
  • Once the timer starts, stand naturally and let the camera take the series.
