Shot 02 | Day 3 | September 19 | Banff

Lake Minnewanka - Layered Landscape

Build the picture in layers: one close foreground detail, the lake, the trees, and the mountains beyond.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Walk the shoreline until you can include one close rock, log, or plant without blocking the lake.
  • Keep the mountains high in the frame and let the water connect the foreground to them.
  • Put the focus point on a crisp mountain edge or a tree line across the lake, not on smooth water.
  • Half-press and make sure the far tree line or mountain is sharp before pressing fully.
  • Take one version with the foreground detail and another without it; keep the clearer frame.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp mountain edge or the far tree line, not on smooth water.

Look for: The distant edge under the focus box should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Foreground is distractingStep sideways or remove the close object; the layers should still lead toward the mountains.
Camera will not focusAim at a dark-light edge on the far tree line or mountain, not the lake surface.
Sky is washed outTry exposure compensation at -1/3, then compare it with the frame at 0.
Whole picture is blurryBrace the camera and retake. If the shutter speed is below 1/125, raise ISO.

Tips

  • A small foreground object gives the scene depth, but keep it simple.
  • If the water is choppy, make the mountains and tree line the main shapes instead of waiting for a reflection.
