Shot 39 | Day 9 | September 25 | Athabasca Falls, Jasper National Park

Athabasca Falls - Blurred Water With 10-Stop ND

Keep the rocks sharp while letting the moving water blur.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/2 sec | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Put the camera on the tripod and frame the falls before attaching the dark filter.
  • With the 10-stop ND filter off, focus on a crisp rock edge beside the moving water.
  • Once focus is confirmed, switch to manual focus if available, then attach the filter.
  • Use Tv at 1/2 second and take the picture with the 2-second self-timer.
  • Check rocks and water, then try 1/4 second and 1 second.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp textured rock edge beside the waterfall, at about the same distance as the water.

Look for: The rock edge should look sharp. Do not judge focus by moving water; it is supposed to blur.

If this happens

Whole picture is blurrySecure the tripod, use the 2-second timer, and retake.
Water looks too frozenChange from 1/2 second to 1 second.
Water is too smoothChange from 1/2 second to 1/4 second.
Camera will not focus with filter onRemove filter, focus first, switch to manual focus if available, then reinstall.

Tips

  • Take versions at 1/4 second, 1/2 second, and 1 second.
  • If white water becomes featureless, try -1/3 exposure compensation.
