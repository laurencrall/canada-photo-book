Shot 22 | Day 11 | September 27 | Vancouver

Grouse Mountain - Vancouver from Above

Leave a little nearby foreground so the distant city and water have a clear sense of height and place.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Choose a clear viewpoint only if weather makes the city and water visible.
  • Include a little tree line, railing, or mountain edge in the foreground.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp distant building or skyline edge.
  • Half-press and confirm the city edge is sharp.
  • Take one wide frame for the view and one slightly tighter frame for the city.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp distant building or skyline edge.

Look for: The city edge should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

City is hazyMake the foreground and weather part of the picture rather than forcing the distant view.
Camera will not focusAim at a crisp distant building edge or nearer railing.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare.
View feels flatInclude a little nearby foreground for depth.

Tips

  • Only make this page a priority when the city view is clear.
  • Do not force both Stanley Park and Grouse if travel fatigue is high.
