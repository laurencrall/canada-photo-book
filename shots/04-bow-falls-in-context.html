Shot 04 | Day 5 | September 21 | Banff

Bow Falls in Context

Show the falls as part of the Banff landscape: water below, mountains behind, and a little surrounding river.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Stand where the falls, river, and mountain backdrop all fit without the falls becoming tiny.
  • Leave some river in the foreground so the eye travels toward the falls and mountains.
  • Put the focus point on a crisp rock edge near the falls or a distant mountain edge, not on rushing water.
  • Half-press and check that the edge under the focus box is crisp before pressing fully.
  • Take one level frame, then check the riverbank and horizon before moving on.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp rock edge near the falls or a distant mountain edge, not on rushing water.

Look for: The chosen rock or mountain edge should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Falls are tinyMove a little closer or crop out empty sky, but keep enough river and mountains for context.
Camera will not focusUse a high-contrast rock edge beside the water rather than the moving white water.
Sky is washed outTry exposure compensation at -1/3, then compare with the frame at 0.
Whole picture is blurryBrace the camera. If the shutter speed is below 1/125, raise ISO and retake.

Tips

  • Keep the camera level; a tilted riverbank makes the wide landscape feel unsteady.
  • If people are in the best viewpoint, take several frames from the same spot and choose the cleanest later.
