Bird

Wait for a clean perch, a simple background, and a little space in front of where the bird is looking or moving.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/1000 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Find a bird that is settled; do not chase it from perch to perch.
  • Frame the bird with a little space in front of its beak or movement.
  • Put the AF point on the eye or head.
  • Half-press and take a short sequence while the bird is still.
  • If it takes off, keep watching rather than running after it.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the bird's eye or head.

Look for: The eye or head should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Bird is blurryKeep 1/1000 and brace the camera.
Bird is tinyMake it a bird-in-place picture or crop later; do not chase it.
Camera will not focusAim at the eye, head, or sharp wing edge.
Background is busyWait for a cleaner perch or move only if you can do so quietly.

Tips

  • A still bird on a clean perch often makes the better photo than a distant bird in flight.
  • Leave space in front of the beak so the frame feels less crowded.
