Shot 05 | Day 5 | September 21 | Banff

Banff Gondola - Moving Cabin

Make the gondola cabin the subject and leave open space in front of its direction of travel.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/500 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Watch the cabins for a clear view with the mountains behind them.
  • Place the cabin to one side and leave room in front of where it is moving.
  • Put the focus point on the cabin edge, logo, or a window frame, not on the distant mountains.
  • Half-press and confirm the cabin looks sharp before pressing fully.
  • Take several frames as cabins move through the cleanest part of the background.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the cabin edge, logo, or a window frame, not on the distant mountains.

Look for: The cabin under the focus box should look sharp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Cabin is blurryMake sure Tv shows 1/500 or faster. Use 1/1000 if the cabin still blurs.
Focus lands on mountainsMove the AF point onto a cabin edge or logo and half-press again.
Cabin is too darkTry exposure compensation at +1/3 and compare it with the frame at 0.
Cable or cabin is cut offStep back from the edge of the frame and leave more room in front of the moving cabin.

Tips

  • Take several frames; the best mountain background changes quickly as the cabin moves.
  • Keep hands and camera straps inside the cabin and do not lean out for a picture.
