Shot 48 | Day 5 | September 21 | Northern Lights Alpine Kitchen, Banff Gondola Summit

Dinner With the Mountain View

Make the dinner feel connected to the mountain setting instead of photographing only the food or only the window.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Wait for food or drinks to arrive, before the table becomes cluttered.
  • Include David, part of the table, or a drink or plate with the view behind.
  • Choose an angle with as little window reflection as possible.
  • Focus on David if included; otherwise focus on the main food or drink.
  • Take one version emphasizing the table and another emphasizing more of the view.

Focus this shot

Place it: If David is included, put the AF point on his nearest eye or face; otherwise use the main food or drink.

Look for: The foreground subject should be sharp while the mountain view remains recognizable.

If this happens

David is darkTry +1/3 exposure compensation and take another frame.
Window reflections showShift angle and keep bright indoor lights out of reflection.
Mountains disappearFrame wider so dinner and location are both visible.
Restaurant photo is blurryHold steady, brace against the table if practical, and take several frames.

Tips

  • Take this before used plates, napkins, and glasses cover the table.
  • One photo with a person and one without gives better album choices.
