Building, Hotel, or Train Station

Keep vertical lines straight and give the building one clear shape. Step back until the whole story fits without too much empty pavement.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Stand far enough back to include the important roofline, sign, or entrance.
  • Keep the camera level so the walls do not lean inward.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp sign, window frame, or building edge.
  • Half-press and confirm the edge is sharp.
  • Take one full-building frame and one detail of the sign or entrance.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp sign, window frame, or building edge.

Look for: The selected edge should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Building leansKeep the camera level and step back rather than tilting up.
Scene is busyWait for people to clear or crop out unnecessary pavement.
Camera will not focusAim at a sign, window frame, or crisp edge.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare.

Tips

  • A strong sign or entrance often tells the travel story better than the entire facade.
  • Make one clean level frame before experimenting.
