Shot 15 | Day 9 | September 25 | Icefields Parkway

Peyto Lake - Full Recognizable Shape

Make sure Peyto Lake's recognizable shape is fully visible and not cut off at the edges.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Use the main viewpoint and check that the full lake shape fits in the frame.
  • Keep the lake as the main subject with forest and mountains only for context.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp far-shore or mountain edge, not on the lake surface.
  • Half-press and confirm the distant edge is sharp.
  • Take a second frame slightly wider before leaving the viewpoint.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp far-shore or mountain edge, not on the lake surface.

Look for: The distant edge should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Lake is cut offWiden the view until the recognizable shape fits cleanly.
Camera will not focusUse a far-shore or mountain edge rather than water.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare.
View is crowdedTake several frames from the same spot and choose the cleanest later.

Tips

  • Peyto Lake is a priority stop, so give it enough time.
  • Check the edges before shooting; the lake shape is the picture.
