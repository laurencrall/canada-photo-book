Shot 07 | Day 7 | September 23 | Lake Louise

Lake Louise - Classic View

Keep the lake level and let the glacier and mountains stay the clear center of the scene.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Walk the shoreline until the glacier is centered and both side mountains frame it.
  • Keep the lake line level; use only a small amount of shoreline foreground if it helps.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp glacier or mountain edge, not on smooth water.
  • Half-press and confirm that the distant glacier edge looks sharp.
  • Take a second frame if the water is calm enough to show a reflection.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp glacier or mountain edge, not on smooth water.

Look for: The distant glacier edge should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Glacier is not sharpMove the AF point to a crisp glacier or mountain edge and refocus.
Reflection is weakKeep the same scene and make the mountains the main subject rather than waiting for perfect water.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation, then compare with 0.
Picture is blurryBrace the camera. If the shutter speed is below 1/125, raise ISO.

Tips

  • Early or late light is calmer and often gives the lake more shape.
  • Keep the horizon and lake line level; a small tilt is obvious here.
