Couple Photo

Keep the people close enough to feel connected, then give the scenery enough room to explain where the moment happened.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Place them close together and turn both bodies slightly toward the same light.
  • Leave a simple gap above their heads and avoid a pole or tree growing from either head.
  • Put the AF point on the nearer eye.
  • Half-press and wait for focus confirmation before taking a few frames.
  • Take one looking-at-camera frame and one looking-at-the-view frame.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the nearer person's eye.

Look for: The nearer eye should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Faces are softMove the AF point onto the nearer eye and refocus.
Background is distractingMove a few steps sideways or use a cleaner, more distant background.
One face is darkTurn both people toward open sky, away from deep shade.
Picture feels stiffAsk them to look at each other, then take several frames.

Tips

  • Take both a smiling frame and a natural in-between moment.
  • Check that the background is not growing out of anyone's head.
