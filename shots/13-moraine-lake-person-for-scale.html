Moraine Lake - Person for Scale

Keep the person small so the lake and mountains still feel enormous.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Choose a safe viewpoint where one person can stand or walk without blocking the landscape.
  • Keep the person low in the frame and leave the mountains much larger than them.
  • Put the AF point on the person if they are the story, or on a mountain edge if the landscape matters most.
  • Half-press and confirm the person or chosen mountain edge is sharp before pressing fully.
  • Take a few frames while the person changes position slightly; a small shift can make the scale read better.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the person when they are the story, or on a mountain edge when the landscape matters most.

Look for: The chosen subject under the focus box should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Person looks too largeStep back or zoom out slightly so the landscape stays much larger.
Person is blurryPut the AF point on the person and half-press again before they move.
Focus lands on mountainsMove the AF point directly onto the person and refocus.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare.

Tips

  • Use only safe, permitted viewpoints; do not move off the trail for a photo.
  • The person is there for scale, not as a formal portrait.
