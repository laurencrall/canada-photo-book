VIA Rail - Through the Train Window

Make the outside scenery the subject while keeping window reflections as low as possible.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/500 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Move close to the window and turn off interior lights if possible.
  • Hold the lens near the glass without pressing hard against it.
  • Put the AF point on a distant ridge, tree, or building outside the train.
  • Half-press and confirm the outside subject is sharp.
  • Take several frames; train movement and reflections change quickly.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a distant ridge, tree, or building outside the train.

Look for: The outside subject should look sharp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Window reflectionMove closer to glass and shade the lens with your hand.
Outside view is blurryUse 1/500 or faster and brace the camera.
Focus lands insideMove the AF point onto a clear outside edge and refocus.
Window is dirtyChoose a cleaner section of glass or make the view more distant.

Tips

  • The best frame may be a simple travel-story view, not a perfect landscape.
  • Take several pictures when the scenery opens up.
