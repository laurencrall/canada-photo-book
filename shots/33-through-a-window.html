Through a Window

Make the outside view the subject. Get close to the glass and manage reflections rather than trying to make a perfect postcard through every window.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/500 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Move close to the window and turn off interior lights if possible.
  • Keep the lens near the glass without pressing hard against it.
  • Put the AF point on a distant ridge, tree, or building outside.
  • Half-press and confirm the outside subject is sharp.
  • Take several frames as reflections and the view change.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a distant ridge, tree, or building outside.

Look for: The outside subject should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Window reflectionMove closer to glass and shade the lens with your hand.
Outside view is blurryUse 1/500 or faster and brace the camera.
Focus lands insideMove the AF point onto a clear outside edge and refocus.
Window is dirtyChoose a cleaner section or make the outside view more distant.

Tips

  • A little reflection can add a travel-story feeling if it does not hide the view.
  • Take several pictures when the scenery opens up.
