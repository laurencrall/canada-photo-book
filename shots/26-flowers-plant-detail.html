Flowers or Plant Detail

Choose one clean, well-lit bloom or leaf and simplify everything behind it. Details become memorable when the subject is unmistakable.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Choose one flower, leaf, or small cluster with a clean background.
  • Move close enough that the subject fills much of the frame.
  • Put the AF point on the nearest important edge or center of the flower.
  • Half-press and confirm that detail is sharp.
  • Take one frame straight on and one from slightly lower or higher.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the nearest important edge or flower center.

Look for: The main petal edge or center should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Flower is softHold still, refocus on the nearest detail, and take another frame.
Background is busyChange your angle until the background becomes simpler.
Wind moves the flowerWait for a calm moment or raise ISO so the camera uses a faster shutter.
Color looks dullTry open shade or side light instead of direct harsh sun.

Tips

  • Look for an uncluttered background before worrying about the exact flower.
  • Take a second frame with a little more space around the subject.
