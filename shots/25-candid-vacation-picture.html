Candid Vacation Picture

The best candid has a real activity and a simple background. Watch for a moment rather than asking someone to pose.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/250 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Notice a small activity: walking, reading a menu, laughing, or looking at a view.
  • Move to a clean angle before raising the camera.
  • Put the AF point on the person's nearer eye or face.
  • Half-press, then take a short sequence as the moment unfolds.
  • Review only after the moment has passed so you do not interrupt it.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the person's nearer eye or face.

Look for: The face should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Person is blurryUse 1/500 if they are walking quickly or moving more.
Moment looks posedStep back and wait for the activity to restart.
Background competesChange your angle so the background is simpler.
Faces are darkMove toward open shade or turn slightly toward brighter sky.

Tips

  • Take several frames quietly; the best expression is often between posed moments.
  • Keep the camera at the ready rather than making the moment wait.
