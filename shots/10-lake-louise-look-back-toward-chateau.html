Shot 10 | Day 7 | September 23 | Lake Louise

Lake Louise - Look Back Toward Chateau

After the classic lake view, turn around and make the Chateau the recognizable subject with the lake around it.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Turn away from the glacier view and find a clean view of the Chateau across the water.
  • Include enough lake and mountains to show the setting, but keep the Chateau recognizable.
  • Put the AF point on a high-contrast edge of the Chateau, not on water or sky.
  • Half-press and confirm the building edge is sharp before pressing fully.
  • Take one level frame, then look for distracting poles, people, or branches at the edges.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a high-contrast edge of the Chateau, not on water or sky.

Look for: The building edge under the focus box should look sharp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Chateau is too smallZoom in a little or remove empty sky while keeping enough lake for context.
Camera will not focusAim at a window, roof, or building edge rather than the water.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare with 0.
Building looks tiltedKeep the camera level and step back before tilting it upward.

Tips

  • This is the alternate view many people miss after photographing the glacier.
  • Keep building lines level; small tilts make the Chateau look like it is leaning.
