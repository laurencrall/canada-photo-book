Moraine Lake - Mountain Detail

If one peak or ridge stands out, make that detail the picture instead of trying to include everything.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Look across the range for one peak, line, or patch of light that stands out.
  • Zoom until the detail fills most of the frame and remove extra empty sky or lake.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp ridge edge where light and dark meet.
  • Half-press and confirm the chosen ridge looks sharp before pressing fully.
  • Take a second frame slightly wider in case the tight crop feels crowded later.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp ridge edge where light and dark meet.

Look for: The ridge under the focus box should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Detail feels tinyZoom in or crop out empty sky; you do not need the whole lake.
Camera will not focusAim at a high-contrast ridge edge instead of the sky.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare.
Picture is blurryBrace the camera and raise ISO if the shutter speed is below 1/125.

Tips

  • A tight mountain detail makes a useful partner to the classic wide view.
  • Keep one clean ridge line as the clear subject.
