Shot 20 | Day 11 | September 27 | Vancouver

Stanley Park - Lions Gate Bridge

Use the seawall or shoreline to lead toward the Lions Gate Bridge, with enough water to show the setting.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Find a spot where the bridge is clearly visible beyond the seawall or shoreline.
  • Let the path or water edge lead toward the bridge instead of cutting across it.
  • Put the AF point on a bridge tower or cable edge.
  • Half-press and confirm the bridge edge is sharp.
  • Take a level frame and check that the bridge is not hidden by trees or poles.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a bridge tower or cable edge.

Look for: The bridge edge should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Bridge is tinyZoom in or move to a clearer waterfront view.
Camera will not focusUse a bridge tower or cable edge rather than water.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare.
Frame feels busySimplify the foreground and keep the bridge as the clear subject.

Tips

  • Keep both the waterfront and bridge visible.
  • A little seawall or path gives the picture a sense of place.
