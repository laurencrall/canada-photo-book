The We Were Here Sign

Make the place name easy to read so the picture immediately tells you where you were.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Look for an official park, town, lake, station, hotel, or attraction sign.
  • Stand where the full name can be read without anything blocking important words.
  • Include a little surrounding scenery so it is more than lettering.
  • Put the AF point on the place name or a crisp sign edge.
  • Take a clean sign picture and, if convenient, one with travelers beside it.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the printed place name or a crisp sign edge.

Look for: The important words should look clearly sharp and readable.

If this happens

Sign is blurryMove the AF point onto the lettering and refocus.
Sign fills the pictureStep back to include scenery or location context.
People walk in frontTake several frames as people move.
Sign is darkTry +1/3 exposure compensation.

Tips

  • Photograph official place names whenever they naturally appear.
  • Take the sign before leaving the location.
