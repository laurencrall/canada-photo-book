E-Bike Tour - Rider in Motion

Keep the rider sharp and include enough scenery that the photo still feels like Banff, not just a bike picture.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/500 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Pick a safe spot with an attractive background where the rider can pass naturally.
  • Stand still and frame before the rider enters.
  • Leave space in front of the rider so they move into the frame.
  • Put the AF point on the rider's upper body or helmet.
  • Take several photos as the rider moves through the frame.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the rider's chest, upper body, or helmet.

Look for: The rider should look sharp and clear as they move through the frame.

If this happens

Rider is blurryIncrease shutter speed to 1/1000 and try again.
Focus is on backgroundMove the AF point back onto rider and take another short sequence.
Rider is too smallZoom in a little or wait until rider is closer.
Photo lacks placeReframe to include mountains, lake, trees, or road.

Tips

  • Take a short burst instead of one frame.
  • A side or slight diagonal angle usually looks better than head-on.
