Bad Weather, Fog, or Clouds

Let the weather be the subject. Fog, rain, and clouds make the picture feel like the real trip when you simplify the scene.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Choose one strong shape: a tree, road, mountain edge, or person with an umbrella.
  • Leave some open fog or sky around it so the weather reads clearly.
  • Put the AF point on the nearest crisp edge, not on blank fog.
  • Half-press and confirm the edge is sharp.
  • Take one frame slightly brighter and compare it to the first.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the nearest crisp edge, not on blank fog.

Look for: The selected edge should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Fog looks grayTry +1/3 exposure compensation and compare.
Camera will not focusAim at a nearer tree, sign, or crisp edge.
Scene feels emptyAdd one strong foreground shape or person.
Rain spots showWipe the front of the lens and take another frame.

Tips

  • Bad weather can make the most distinctive travel photos.
  • Keep the camera dry and wipe the lens often.
