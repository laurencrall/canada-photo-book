Wildlife

Give wildlife room in the frame and in real life. A safe, distant photo with its environment is better than one made by getting closer.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1/500 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Stop where you are; do not approach or call to the animal.
  • Use the longest zoom you have and include some habitat around it.
  • Put the AF point on the eye, head, or highest-contrast part of the animal.
  • Half-press and confirm focus before taking a short sequence.
  • If the animal moves away, let it go and keep your distance.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the eye, head, or highest-contrast edge.

Look for: The eye or head should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Animal is too smallKeep the environmental picture or crop later; do not move closer.
Animal is blurryUse 1/1000 if it is moving quickly.
Camera will not focusAim at the eye, head, or a sharp fur edge.
Animal changes behaviorBack away and stop photographing if your presence affects it.

Tips

  • With the 70mm lens, make an environmental wildlife picture and crop later if needed.
  • The photo is never worth disturbing the animal or stepping off a safe path.
