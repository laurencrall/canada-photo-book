Food or Drinks

Pick one hero dish or drink, simplify the table around it, and show just enough place setting to make it feel like part of the trip.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/5.6 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Move the plate or glass toward window light if possible.
  • Get close enough that one dish or drink is clearly the subject.
  • Put the AF point on the nearest important garnish, rim, or label.
  • Half-press and confirm the key detail is sharp.
  • Take one wider table scene and one close detail.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on the nearest important garnish, rim, or label.

Look for: The key detail should look crisp when focus confirms.

If this happens

Photo is too darkMove closer to a window or brighten exposure slightly.
Table is busyRemove napkins, packets, and extra items from the edges.
Focus is softRefocus on the nearest important detail.
Glass has glareTurn the glass a little or change your angle.

Tips

  • Window light almost always looks better than overhead restaurant light.
  • Take the quick photo before the food gets cold.
