Water Flowing Around Rocks With 10-Stop ND

Let moving water blur, but keep the rocks crisp and clearly defined.

Start here

Tv | Shutter 1 sec | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Find water flowing around rocks, not a flat pool.
  • Frame rocks as an important part of the picture.
  • Focus before attaching the 10-stop ND filter.
  • Put the AF point on a textured edge of the main rock.
  • Attach the filter and try a few shutter speeds.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp edge or textured surface of the main rock.

Look for: The rock should look sharp when focus confirms; moving water may blur.

If this happens

Water is not blurred enoughTry 2 seconds.
Water is too smoothTry 1/2 second instead.
Rock is blurryRefocus on rock before putting filter on.
Camera shakesUse 2-second timer and do not touch camera during exposure.

Tips

  • Look for one main rock shape rather than many scattered little rocks.
  • A slightly lower angle can make water flow more obvious.
