Shot 16 | Day 9 | September 25 | Icefields Parkway

Columbia Icefield - Show Scale

Leave one small, recognizable object in the frame so the glacier or icefield looks enormous.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Find a broad view where the icefield fills most of the frame.
  • Include one small person, vehicle, road, or building only if it safely shows scale.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp glacier edge, rock edge, or distant ridge.
  • Half-press and confirm the chosen edge is sharp.
  • Take one wide frame for scale and one closer frame for ice texture.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp glacier edge, rock edge, or distant ridge.

Look for: The chosen edge should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Icefield feels smallInclude a safe small object for scale and keep the ice dominant.
Camera will not focusAim at a sharp ice, rock, or ridge edge.
Ice looks too darkTry +1/3 exposure compensation and compare with 0.
Picture is blurryBrace the camera and raise ISO if speed is below 1/125.

Tips

  • Stay in permitted areas; never walk onto ice for a photograph.
  • Scale matters more here than a perfectly empty frame.
