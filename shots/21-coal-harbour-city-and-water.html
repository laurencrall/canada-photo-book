Shot 21 | Day 11 | September 27 | Vancouver

Coal Harbour - City and Water

Balance water and skyline so the photo clearly feels like Vancouver's waterfront.

Start here

Av | Aperture f/8 | ISO Auto

How to take it

  • Stand where water and skyline are both visible without one crowding out the other.
  • Use a simple boat, railing, or shoreline detail only if it adds foreground context.
  • Put the AF point on a crisp building edge, not on flat water or sky.
  • Half-press and confirm the skyline is sharp.
  • Take one wide frame and one slightly tighter skyline frame.

Focus this shot

Place it: Put the AF point on a crisp building edge, not on flat water or sky.

Look for: The skyline edge should look crisp when the camera confirms focus.

If this happens

Skyline is tinyZoom in slightly while leaving enough water for context.
Camera will not focusAim at a high-contrast building edge.
Sky is washed outTry -1/3 exposure compensation and compare.
Picture feels emptyInclude one simple foreground boat, railing, or shoreline detail.

Tips

  • Keep the water and skyline in balance.
  • A busy harbour can still work if one clean shape remains the subject.
