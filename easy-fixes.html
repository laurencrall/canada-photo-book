After the trip

Easy Fixes

Longer, step-by-step help for common problems in your photos. Keep the originals and edit copies.

People walking through the scene

Use Photoshop's Median stack

  1. Put the camera on a tripod and take 6-10 frames without moving it while people walk through.
  2. Photoshop: File > Scripts > Load Files into Stack. Select all layers, then Edit > Auto-Align Layers. Convert them to a Smart Object, then choose Layer > Smart Objects > Stack Mode > Median.
  3. This works best when the scene itself stays still. Inspect railings, water, and edges afterward.

Bright sky, dark mountain

Merge bracketed frames as HDR

  1. From one viewpoint, take a normal frame, a darker frame for the sky, and a brighter frame for the land.
  2. In Lightroom Classic select the frames, then choose Photo > Photo Merge > HDR. Start with Low Deghost.
  3. Keep the final result believable: detail in the sky, but a natural-looking mountain.

One person or a small distraction

Use Lightroom Remove

  1. Open the photo in Lightroom and choose Remove. Pick People for background people, or brush over a small object.
  2. Use Regenerate if the first result looks strange. For an awkward edge, try Clone or Heal instead.
  3. Zoom in before exporting, especially around water, rails, and tree branches.

Crooked or loosely framed

Crop and straighten

  1. Choose Crop, then use Straighten to line up a horizon, shoreline, or building edge.
  2. Keep breathing room around the subject. For a reflection, leave room below the shoreline.

Subject too dark

Edit only the subject

  1. In Lightroom, choose Masks > Select Subject. Raise Shadows and Exposure only on the subject.
  2. If the background is also dark, make a separate mask instead of brightening the whole image.

Hazy, flat, noisy, or slightly soft

Make small adjustments

  1. Try a little Dehaze, then modest Contrast and Texture. Stop before the scene looks crunchy.
  2. Use Denoise sparingly. If the whole frame is blurred by camera movement, choose a sharper frame instead.
